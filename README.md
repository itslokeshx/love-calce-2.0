# ❤️ Love Calculator 2.0

A fun little web app that calculates the **love percentage** between you and your crush 💘.
Just enter two names, click **"Show Love Percent"**, and instantly get your result with a matching **GIF reaction** (happy, sad, funny, or romantic depending on the score).

🔗 **Live Link:** [Love Calculator 2.0](https://itslokeshx.github.io/love-calce-2.0/)

---

## ✨ Features

✅ Enter your name + your crush’s name
✅ Get a random **love percentage** result
✅ Dynamic GIFs displayed based on score
✅ Simple, fun, and interactive
✅ Works on both desktop and mobile
